We’re excited to introduce **pingmind**, your AI-powered assistant for setting reminders — directly from your phone, via WhatsApp.

Whether you're in a hurry, walking down the street, or just want to quickly save something for later, **pingmind is always ready to remind you** — just like a smart friend that never forgets.

## How it works

Using pingmind is as simple as texting a friend:

1. **Sign up** at [pingmind.app](https://pingmind.app)
2. **Enjoy a free 3-day trial**
3. **Add the pingmind number to your contacts**
4. Start creating reminders through WhatsApp 💬

No apps, no downloads, no clutter.

## What can it do?

Right now, pingmind lets you:

- ✅ **Create** a reminder
- ✅ **List** all your upcoming reminders
- ✅ **Update** an existing reminder
- ✅ **Cancel** a reminder

_(One at a time – for now!)_

## What’s coming next?

We’re just getting started. Here's a glimpse of what’s on the roadmap:

- 🔁 **Recurring reminders**
- 📥 **Create multiple reminders at once**
- 📝 **Create simple lists** (for groceries, movies, etc.)

Our goal is to build the **most natural way to manage your memory**, directly from your messaging app — no friction, just flow.

## Try it today

Head over to [pingmind.app](https://pingmind.app), sign up in seconds, enjoy your **3-day free trial**, and start pinging your mind.

Because your brain deserves space to think — not to remember everything.
